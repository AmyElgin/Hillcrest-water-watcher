    Ask for a reference number

    Community reporting guides say to ask for a reference number when you log a fault, then share that number with neighbours so people are not all starting from zero. You can store that number on a log in this demo. That does not submit it to the municipality.

    Why Hillcrest

    On 26 January 2025, eThekwini Municipality started a shutdown of Pipeline 53 so the pipe could be moved while SANRAL upgraded the N3. The city said restrictions would last until 22 February 2025 and listed Hillcrest among the affected areas, along with nearby places such as Crestholme, Waterfall, Kloof, Gillits, Bothas Hill, Shongweni, and Molweni. Umgeni-Uthukela Water had isolated the pipeline, which reduced flow from the DV Harris Water Treatment Plant.

    That notice is history, not a statement about today. This page does not know whether your street has water right now.
